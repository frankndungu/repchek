Reading the screen

Repchek draws a skeleton over your body. Its colour tells you what the coach is thinking, rep by rep.

  • Get in position

    I can see you. Get into your start position and hold still for a second. You hear a whistle, then counting starts. Setting up never costs you reps.

  • Good form

    Your form is in range. Keep going. Every green rep is counted as clean.

  • Fix this now

    Something broke your form, like hips sagging. The coach tells you what to fix, and that rep is marked in your summary so you can rewatch it.

  • I cannot see you

    Part of your body is hidden or out of frame. Nothing is counted or judged until I see you clearly. Move the phone back or turn it side-on.

  • Your back line

    Head to shoulder to hip to knee, on side views. Keep it straight. A bend here is what gets backs hurt on deadlifts, rows and push-ups.
